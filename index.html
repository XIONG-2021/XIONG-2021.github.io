ACADEMIC HOMEPAGE

Jingfeng Xiong

Lower-limb Exoskeletons · Human–Robot Interaction · Intelligent Control

I work on lower-limb exoskeleton systems, with research interests in locomotion-mode recognition, gait-state estimation, assistance control, and multi-sensor fusion. My work examines methods for modelling and controlling wearable robotic systems in walking tasks.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

Research focus

Lower-limb exoskeletons

Perception, prediction, and control methods for lower-limb exoskeletons across level walking, stair negotiation, and other locomotion tasks.

Learning-based gait modelling

Data-driven approaches for locomotion-mode recognition, gait-phase estimation, joint-moment estimation, and motion-state prediction.

Human–robot interaction

Integration of wearable sensors, kinematics, and environmental information for real-time state estimation and assistance control.
